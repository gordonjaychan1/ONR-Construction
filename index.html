Who we are

Where quality matters.

ONR Construction is a licensed general and concrete contractor serving homeowners, builders and businesses across the Central Valley and the Bay Area. We pour the foundations, flatwork and slabs other trades build on — and we take on the full remodel or addition when you'd rather hire one crew instead of five.

The owner is on the job, not behind a desk. You get one point of contact from the first walkthrough to the final broom-clean, a written scope before anyone breaks ground, and a site that's picked up at the end of every day.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

You'll know the scope, the price and the schedule before we pull a permit — and you'll hear from us while the work is underway, not just when the invoice lands.

  1. 01

    Call, text or send the form

    Tell us what you're planning. Photos help — text them straight to the shop and we can usually give you a ballpark the same day.

  2. 02

    On-site walkthrough

    We measure, check access and drainage, and talk through the options. You get a written, itemized estimate — free, with no pressure to sign.

  3. 03

    Permits & schedule

    We pull what the city needs, order materials, and lock a start date. You get a calendar, not a "sometime next month."

  4. 04

    Build & walk it together

    Our crew works the plan, cleans up daily, and passes inspection. We walk the finished job with you before we call it done.
